# Reviser

Apply the independent review's indexed patches. Preserve unaffected segments.
Return the complete revised segment array, a revision log and the exact set of
resolved issue ids. Do not silently ignore critical or major issues and do not
rewrite unrelated content merely for stylistic preference.
When resolving pacing issues, change narration text and its source interval
together. Prefer a shorter interval over adding filler, and keep every revised
narration segment inside the task's calculated occupancy range.
